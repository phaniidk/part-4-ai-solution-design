# AI Solution Report

## 1. Business Domain

Retail

---

## 2. Business Problem

Businesses face customer loss due to competition and poor customer engagement. Predicting churn helps improve retention.

---

## 3. AI Task Type

Classification

The system predicts whether a customer is likely to churn or not.

---

## 4. Data Requirement Plan

### Required Data
- Customer purchase history
- Customer activity data
- Customer demographics

### Input Features
- Purchase frequency
- Spending amount
- Customer engagement

### Target Variable
- Churn status

### Data Risks
- Missing data
- Imbalanced classes
- Incorrect customer records

---

## 5. Model Recommendation

Feed-forward Neural Network

This model works well for structured business data and classification problems.

---

## 6. Evaluation Plan

### Technical Metrics
- Accuracy
- Precision
- Recall
- F1-score

### Business Metrics
- Customer retention improvement
- Reduction in churn rate

---

## 7. Responsible AI Considerations

- Bias in customer data
- Incorrect predictions
- Need for human validation
- Privacy protection

---

## 8. Final Solution Summary

The proposed AI system predicts customer churn using customer-related business data. The solution can help businesses improve customer retention and decision-making.
